## Purpose

Explain the firm's confirmed legal services with useful, distinct guidance for individuals and businesses, without implying guarantees or offering work the firm does not provide.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Individual services routes
The site SHALL provide `/particulares/` and these service routes: `/particulares/derecho-civil/`, `/particulares/derecho-laboral/`, `/particulares/familia/`, `/particulares/sucesiones/`, `/particulares/danos-y-perjuicios/`, and `/particulares/contratos-y-alquileres/`.

#### Scenario: Individual visitor explores a service
- **WHEN** a visitor opens an individual service route
- **THEN** the page describes the relevant service in plain Argentine Spanish, gives a clear way to request contact, and does not guarantee a legal outcome

### Requirement: Business services routes
The site SHALL provide `/empresas/` and routes for `/empresas/asesoramiento-permanente/`, `/empresas/derecho-laboral/`, `/empresas/contratos-comerciales/`, and `/empresas/cobro-de-creditos/`.

#### Scenario: Business visitor explores ongoing counsel
- **WHEN** a business visitor opens `/empresas/` or `/empresas/asesoramiento-permanente/`
- **THEN** the page gives prominent, concrete information about monthly retainers, preventive counsel, business labor law, commercial contracts, debt recovery, conflict prevention, judicial representation, and corporate advice, without inventing fees or claiming the service is free

### Requirement: Business service prominence
The home page and `/empresas/` SHALL make ongoing legal advice for SMEs and medium-sized businesses a prominent service with a clear contact path.

#### Scenario: Business visitor looks for ongoing counsel
- **WHEN** a business visitor scans the home page or opens `/empresas/`
- **THEN** ongoing counsel and monthly retainers are easy to find and are described without fabricated pricing or outcome guarantees

### Requirement: Useful service content
Every published service page SHALL contain distinct information relevant to its subject and SHALL be reviewed by the firm's lawyers before publication.

#### Scenario: Service content is ready for release
- **WHEN** a service page is selected for publication
- **THEN** the content has an identified professional reviewer, avoids unsupported legal claims, and explains that general information does not replace individualized professional advice

### Requirement: Excluded practice areas
The site SHALL NOT advertise criminal or pension law services.

#### Scenario: Visitor browses service listings
- **WHEN** service cards, menus, or service pages are displayed
- **THEN** criminal law and pension law do not appear as services offered by the firm
